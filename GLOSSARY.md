# Glossary — Top Doctors Khulna

**Doctor**
A medical practitioner listed in the directory. Has exactly one primary Specialty and may also be listed under additional Specialties.
_Avoid_: Specialist (as a noun for the person), Consultant

**Specialty**
A field of medicine used to group Doctors (e.g. Cardiology, ENT). The person-facing label ("Cardiologist") is a display form of the Specialty, not a separate concept.
_Avoid_: Category, Speciality, Department

**Primary Specialty**
The one Specialty a Doctor is principally known for; decides where the Doctor's own page lives.

**Designation**
A Doctor's academic or professional rank (Professor, Associate Professor, Assistant Professor, Consultant, Specialist, Medical Officer, General Physician). One per Doctor; used to browse Doctors by seniority.
_Avoid_: Category, Rank, Title

**Featured Doctor**
A Doctor chosen to appear in the "Popular Doctors" section of the home page. An editorial choice, not a measure of quality.

**Chamber**
A place where a Doctor sees patients, with an address and a weekly Schedule. A Doctor may have several Chambers.
_Avoid_: Clinic, Practice, Office

**Schedule**
The days and time ranges a Doctor sits at a given Chamber, as last published. Not a guarantee of availability — patients are told to call to confirm.

**Consultation Fee**
What a patient pays to see a Doctor. Has three parts: New Patient fee, Returning Patient fee, and Report Showing fee (often free).
_Avoid_: Visit fee, Charge

**Availability**
A published flag saying whether a Doctor is currently taking patients. Shown with low emphasis because it may be stale.

**Facility**
A non-doctor listing in the directory: a Hospital, Diagnostic Centre, Pharmacy, Blood Bank, or Ambulance service.

**Service**
Something the site's operator itself provides to patients via the Booking Line: doctor appointment booking, lab test discount, hospital admission support, ambulance, and treatment-in-India assistance. Distinct from a Facility, which is a third party listed in the directory.

**Post**
An article on the site's blog, written to bring patients searching for care in Khulna to a Doctor or Service. Every Post is published in both Bangla and English. A Post is a Health Guide, a Doctor List or a test-price comparison. Every Post opens with a short direct answer and ends with an FAQ.
_Avoid_: Article, Blog (for a single piece)

**Health Guide**
A Post that explains a health problem or a practical task (which specialist to see, how to book a serial) and points the reader to the right Specialty or Service.

**Doctor List**
A Post presenting a group of Doctors, generated from the directory: every Doctor in one Specialty ("best X in Khulna"), every Doctor with a Chamber at one Hospital ("doctor list & serial number"), or every Doctor who sits on a given day (Friday). Doctors appear in seniority order and are never numbered or ranked: the order is not a measure of quality. Each Specialty has exactly one Doctor List; the Specialty's own page links to it and does not compete for the "best" search.
_Avoid_: Top 10, Ranking

**Test Price**
A diagnostic centre's price for a lab test, as confirmed with the centre on a stated date. Only confirmed prices are published; a test with none has no Post.
_Avoid_: Estimate, Rate

**Medical Reviewer**
A Doctor named on a Post as having checked its medical content. Lends the Post credibility; does not make it medical advice.
_Avoid_: Author (the Medical Reviewer need not have written the Post)

**Booking Line**
The single site-wide phone/WhatsApp number patients use to request an appointment with any Doctor. Not the Doctor's or Chamber's own number.
_Avoid_: Doctor's phone, Hotline (ambiguous)
